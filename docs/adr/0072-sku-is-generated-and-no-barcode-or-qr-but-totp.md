# ADR-0072: A product is known by the SKU the server generates; no barcode, and no QR code but TOTP enrolment's

- **Status:** Accepted (owner, 2026-11-17: the decisions; B09q built them)
- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Session:** B09q
- **Deciders:** owner; backend track built it

## Context

Until contract 1.54.0 a product carried a `sku` typed by whoever created it and an optional `barcode`, unique among live products; `listProducts` looked a product up by `barcode`; the planned offline pack gave the till each product's `barcode` to scan; `getProductLabel` drew a `CODE_128` of the product's barcode or SKU and `getStockUnitLabel` one of `<SKU>/<batch or tag>` (B05x, A-151). The product import sheet took `sku` and `barcode` columns, and a new SKU in the sheet created a product (A-130). The TOTP enrolment QR (ADR-0037) was the only QR code built; B9b's opener planned another, the receipt link shown as a QR code at the counter (CR-033 point 2).

The owner decided on 2026-11-17 (B09q opener, decisions 1 to 3):

1. The TOTP enrolment QR is the only QR code; nothing else draws one.
2. No product barcode: the field, its unique index, the import column, the barcode lookup and till scanning go, and so does the barcode on the product and the lot or animal labels, which keep their printed text (SKU, lot code, tag, dates, weight). The meat pack labels (PRC, migration 0085, `printPackLabels`) stay exactly as built, barcode included: meat is future work.
3. A SKU is generated by the server at creation: a short prefix from the product's category and a zero-padded number (`EGG-0001`), unique, never reused (its own sequence per prefix, never reset), never edited, never accepted as input on create, update or import (a request carrying it is 422, not silently ignored). Changing a product's category never changes its SKU.

The owner approved the breaking contract version 2.0.0 for these removals. Left undecided, a SKU typed by hand can be reused after an archive (the old index was live rows only), mistyped or guessed from another product's, and a barcode field keeps a scanning path the counter is not meant to have.

No accepted ADR set the SKU as input or the product barcode; A-130 (the product sheet) and A-151 (the label barcodes) were assumptions, and are superseded in part (A-248 to A-251).

## Options considered

1. **A per-prefix counter in the database, allocated in the creating transaction (`mdm.allocate_sku`).** One row per prefix, moved forward only, by a `SECURITY DEFINER` function; a rollback returns the number, so the only numbers ever seen are those of products that exist. Costs a function, a table and a trigger, and serialises product creates of one prefix.
2. **A PostgreSQL sequence per prefix.** No table to guard, but a sequence per category prefix must be created by DDL at runtime (the application role has none, ADR-0024), and a sequence is not transactional: a refused create burns a number for good.
3. **A random or hashed SKU.** No counter at all, but unreadable at the counter and on a label, which the owner's format (`EGG-0001`) rules out.

For the barcode: **(a)** drop the column in this release, or **(b)** expand then contract: stop reading and writing it and drop its unique index now, and drop the column in the first migration after 2.0.0 is deployed, as docs/DATA_MODEL.md requires for a column the previous API version still reads.

## Decision

Option 1, and (b) for the barcode.

- **Prefix.** `mdm.category.sku_prefix` (`^[A-Z]{2,5}$`, NOT NULL): given on `createCategory` or `updateCategory` as the optional `skuPrefix`, or else filled by the trigger `mdm.category_sku_prefix_default` from the first three letters of the code (`CAT` when it has fewer than two); existing categories are backfilled the same way (migration 0210). A new prefix applies to products made after it.
- **Counter.** `mdm.sku_counter` (`class=PROJECTION`: `hk_app` and `hk_worker` read only), one row per prefix; `mdm.sku_counter_forward` refuses lowering, deleting or truncating it for every role (HK004 `ck_sku_counter_forward`).
- **Allocation.** `mdm.allocate_sku(prefix)`, `SECURITY DEFINER`, owned by `hk_owner`, fixed `search_path`: upserts the counter by one, steps over any number a product already holds (a SKU typed before 0210), and returns `<PREFIX>-<number>`, four digits at least and never truncated. `ProductCatalogue.createIn` calls it through `ProductStore.allocateSku` once the product is valid, in the transaction that inserts it; `createProduct` takes the advisory lock `mdm.sku` (`SKU_SERIES_LOCK`, `seriesLocks`) before its transaction begins, so concurrent creates queue rather than retry on the counter.
- **Never reused, never edited.** `uq_product_sku` is unique over every product, live or archived; `mdm.product_sku_fixed` refuses any change to `sku` for every role (HK004 `ck_product_sku_fixed`).
- **Never input.** `SERVER_ASSIGNED_PRODUCT_FIELDS = ['sku']` and `serverAssignedFieldsIn` (MDM domain); `createProduct`, `updateProduct`, `createIn` and `updateIn` refuse an input that carries `sku`, whatever its value (null and empty included), with 422 `FIELD_SERVER_ASSIGNED` (`/sku`, `SERVER_ASSIGNED`), after authorisation and before anything is written. The boundary schema lets `sku` through only so the handler can refuse it; the contract does not list it. The product sheet keeps its `sku` column: a SKU the catalogue holds names the product to change, a blank one creates a product with a generated SKU, and a SKU the catalogue does not hold is a row error.
- **No barcode.** `barcode` leaves `Product`, `ProductCreate`, `ProductUpdate`, `ProductLabel`, `StockUnitLabel` and `OfflineProduct`, and `listProducts`' parameters; a request that still sends `barcode` to `listProducts` is 400 (`/barcode`) rather than answered with the unfiltered catalogue, and in a body it is 400 as an unknown field. `mdm.product.barcode` is neither read nor written; migration 0211 drops `uq_product_barcode`; the column goes in the first migration after 2.0.0 is deployed (BL-075, A-249). A product sheet with a `barcode` column fails as having an unknown column.
- **Labels.** `productLabel` (MDM domain) and `stockUnitLabel` (INV domain) build the label's text field by field, with no barcode. The meat pack labels (`createPacks`, `listPacks`, `printPackLabels`, `prc.pack.barcode`, `LabelBarcode`) and the planned picking of a pack by its barcode (`PickInput.packBarcode`, `FulfilmentLine.packBarcode`, B11) are untouched.
- **QR codes.** The TOTP enrolment QR (`UqrQrCodes`, ADR-0037) is the only one. ESLint refuses an import of a QR or barcode library (`uqr`, `qrcode`, `jsqr`, `@zxing/*`, `bwip-js`, `jsbarcode` and the like) anywhere in `src` but `src/modules/iam/infrastructure/uqr-qr-codes.ts` and its spec; tests may decode the TOTP QR. B9b's receipt link reaches the customer by SMS, never a QR code (ADR-0026 Amendments, 2026-11-17).

## Consequences

- A SKU is readable, unique over the catalogue's whole history and never changes, so a label, a statement or a stock count printed years apart names the same product; the seed and the tests name products by the SKUs the server gives them in seed order (`expectSeededSku`).
- A client can no longer choose, edit or reuse a SKU, nor send a barcode; clients written against 1.x that send `sku` get 422 and must drop it. The frontend's labels screen prints text only (CR-016's interim Code 128 drawing goes).
- Product creates queue on one advisory lock; at the farm's volume this costs nothing, and imports, which create many products in one transaction, take no lock and retry on a rare conflict.
- Forbidden: a migration or a role that lowers, deletes or truncates `mdm.sku_counter`; an `UPDATE` of `mdm.product.sku`; any QR or barcode library outside the TOTP adapter; any new read or write of `mdm.product.barcode`.
- Binds `docs/data-model/mdm.md` (`category`, `product`, `sku_counter`), `docs/API.md` (MDM, INV, versioning), `docs/MODULE_BOUNDARIES.md` (the MDM and INV cards), `contract/openapi.yaml` 2.0.0 and `docs/CONTRACT_CHANGELOG.md`, and B9b's opener (no QR, no scanning).

## Verification

- `test/integration/mdm/sku-schema.integration.spec.ts`: the prefix given and derived, a malformed prefix refused, `EGG-0001` then `EGG-0002` with another prefix on its own counter, a SKU in use stepped over, past 9999 untruncated, the counter never lowered, deleted or truncated and unwritable by `hk_app`, fifty concurrent allocations fifty different numbers, an archived product's SKU refused, any change to a SKU refused by every role, the barcode index gone. Mutation checks: a counter that does not move turned five of its tests red; dropping the step-over and keeping the live-only index turned three red.
- `test/integration/mdm/products.integration.spec.ts`: the generated SKU in the answer, the audit and the event; a SKU sent on create or update (any value, the product's own included) refused with 422 and nothing written; eight concurrent creates eight consecutive SKUs; a category move keeps the SKU; an archived product's number never given again; a category's prefix given, derived and changed for later products only; `barcode` refused on create, update and as a lookup. Mutation check: letting a SKU through turned two red.
- `test/integration/mdm/import-sheets.integration.spec.ts`: a blank SKU creates with a generated one, an unknown SKU is a row error, a `barcode` column fails the sheet. `test/integration/inv/labels.integration.spec.ts`: both labels answer exactly their text keys. Unit specs of `productLabel`, `stockUnitLabel` and `serverAssignedFieldsIn`.
- `test/contract/b9/ids-barcodes.contract.spec.ts`: no operation or schema but `startTotpEnrolment` and `TotpEnrolment` names a QR code; a barcode only on the meat pack and picking operations, elsewhere only in a description saying it went in 2.0.0; the meat pack operations and every component they reach equal contract 1.54.0's byte for byte; every example of every operation valid against its schema. `pnpm lint` (two lint fixtures prove the QR rule fires in application and domain code).
