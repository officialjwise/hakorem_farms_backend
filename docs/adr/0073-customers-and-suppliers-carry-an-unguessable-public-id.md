# ADR-0073: Customers and suppliers carry an unguessable public ID in place of their sequential numbers

- **Status:** Accepted (owner, 2026-11-17: the decision; B09q built it)
- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Session:** B09q
- **Deciders:** owner; backend track built it

## Context

Until contract 1.54.0 a customer answered `customerNo` (`C-00001`, from `mdm.customer_no_seq`) and a supplier `supplierNo` (`S-0001`), in `Customer`, `CustomerRef`, `CustomerProfile`, `Supplier` and `SupplierRef` across 98 operations; the customer and supplier sheets named the record to change by that number. A sequential number tells anyone who sees one how many customers the farm has and what the neighbouring numbers are; it invites counting through them, and one typed on a call is easy to mistype into another real customer's.

`docs/API.md` section 4 already keeps ids (UUIDv7) out of people's sight: documents show their number, animals their tag. The owner decided on 2026-11-17 (B09q opener, decision 4): UUIDs stay as primary keys and in API paths and are never shown to people; customers and suppliers get a public ID, a type prefix (`cus_`, `sup_`) and at least 16 characters of Crockford base32 from a cryptographically secure generator (no 0/O or 1/I confusion), for example `cus_8F3K2M9Q7X4T1V6B`, unique by a database constraint with a retry on collision, never reused, never edited, shown in responses and accepted in search. It replaces the sequential numbers in the API from 2.0.0; their columns stay internally (master data is never hard-deleted); existing rows are backfilled by migration. Readable codes stay as they are: document numbers, lot codes, animal tags, branch codes. The owner approved contract 2.0.0 for it.

Decision 5 of the same opener (no identifier is ever a key: every read authorised whatever identifier it is given, and every link sent to a customer carries its own long random token, each proved by a test) is built in B09q continued (ADR-0026 Amendments, 2026-11-17).

## Options considered

1. **Generated by the application, with the database as backstop.** `PublicIds.next` draws 16 bytes from Node's `randomBytes` and spells them; the insert runs in a savepoint and a collision on the unique constraint is tried again with a fresh ID. The column also defaults to `mdm.new_public_id`, which backfills existing rows and serves the previous API version and fixtures. Costs two spellings of one alphabet (TypeScript and SQL), each tested.
2. **Generated by the database only** (the column default). One generator, but a collision would fail the whole command, and a retry cannot be proved without a way to make the generator collide.
3. **A keyed hash of the UUID** (an HMAC, as the tracking token of ADR-0070). Nothing stored, but the ID would be derivable from the UUID by anyone holding the key and could never be rotated without changing every ID.

## Decision

Option 1.

- **Format.** `PUBLIC_ID_ALPHABET` `0123456789ABCDEFGHJKMNPQRSTVWXYZ` (Crockford's: no I, L, O, U); `publicIdFrom(kind, bytes)` writes `cus_` or `sup_` and one character from the low five bits of each of 16 bytes, so every character is equally likely and the ID carries 80 bits. `readPublicId(kind, text)` reads one typed loosely (any case, spaces and hyphens ignored, O read as 0, I and L as 1) into its one written form, or null (MDM domain, `public-id.ts`, 100 percent coverage gate).
- **Columns** (migration 0212). `mdm.customer.public_id` and `mdm.supplier.public_id`: NOT NULL, the pattern `^cus_[0-9A-HJKMNP-TV-Z]{16}$` or `^sup_…` (`ck_customer_public_id`, `ck_supplier_public_id`), unique over every row ever made (`uq_customer_public_id`, `uq_supplier_public_id`), never changed by any role (HK004 `ck_customer_public_id_fixed`, `ck_supplier_public_id_fixed`). The default `mdm.new_public_id(prefix)` takes the wholly random bytes of `gen_random_uuid()` and five bits of each; adding the column with this volatile default backfilled every existing row with its own ID without updating any row. `customer_no` and `supplier_no` keep being allocated and stored, and are no longer shown.
- **Generation.** `PublicIds` (an MDM provider): `next(kind)` from `randomBytes(16)`; `insertWithFresh(tx, kind, insert)` runs the insert in a savepoint and, on a 23505 from the kind's unique constraint, rolls back to it and tries a fresh ID, up to `PUBLIC_ID_ATTEMPTS` (five), then `PublicIdsExhaustedError` (500, nothing written). `PartiesAdmin.createCustomerIn` and `ReferenceDataAdmin.createSupplierIn` insert through it.
- **The API.** `CustomerPublicId` and `SupplierPublicId` (the patterns); `publicId` replaces `customerNo` in `Customer`, `CustomerRef` and `CustomerProfile` and `supplierNo` in `Supplier` and `SupplierRef`, so every module's answer that names a customer or a supplier shows it (`MasterDataRefs` gives `publicId` in `CustomerFacts` and `SupplierFacts`). A request that sends `publicId` to a create or update is 400 as an unknown field. `listCustomers` and `listSuppliers` take a public ID, typed any way, in `q`; a sequential number finds nothing. The customer and supplier sheets name the record to change by `publicId`; their exports write it.
- **Internal only.** The event `mdm.SupplierRegistered` v1 keeps `supplierNo` (an internal contract, not the API); the seed keeps its customers' numbers as its own handles.

## Consequences

- People see and type an ID that cannot be guessed, counted through or mistyped into another real record's; support finds a customer by it in one search.
- Every client of the 98 operations that showed `customerNo` or `supplierNo` must read `publicId`; the frontend's mock shows deterministic example IDs, one per customer or supplier across every example.
- An ID is unique and permanent, so an archived customer's ID is never given to another.
- Knowing an ID still reads nothing without authorisation: B09q continued proves it operation by operation, with every customer link given its own random token.
- Forbidden: showing a UUID or a sequential number to people as a customer's or supplier's reference; editing a public ID; a public ID built from anything but a secure random source.
- Binds `docs/data-model/mdm.md` (`customer`, `supplier`), `docs/API.md` sections 4 and 18 (MDM), the MDM card of `docs/MODULE_BOUNDARIES.md`, `contract/openapi.yaml` 2.0.0 and `docs/CONTRACT_CHANGELOG.md`.

## Verification

- `src/modules/mdm/domain/public-id.spec.ts`: the owner's example spelled from its bytes, each of the 32 characters equally likely over all 256 byte values, no I, L, O or U, fewer than 16 bytes refused, loose typing read back, anything else read as null.
- `test/integration/mdm/public-id-schema.integration.spec.ts`: a row inserted without one gets one of its pattern; two thousand drawn by the database with no repeat and all 32 characters in use; a duplicate refused even against an archived row; the wrong pattern refused; any change refused by every role. Mutation check: a generator that repeats one byte turned one red.
- `test/integration/mdm/public-ids.integration.spec.ts`: a new customer and supplier answer their public ID and no number; six concurrent customers six IDs; search by the ID typed loosely finds exactly the one, a sequential number nothing, and a supplier's ID no customer; a sent public ID is 400; a collision with another's ID is tried again (once and twice), and five collisions give 500 with nothing written. Mutation checks: no retry turned two red; a generator of two values turned five red.
- `test/contract/b9/ids-barcodes.contract.spec.ts`: no `customerNo` or `supplierNo` anywhere in the contract; each of the five shapes requires `publicId` of its pattern; one public ID per id across every example; every example valid against its schema. The import sheet tests name customers and suppliers by `publicId`.
